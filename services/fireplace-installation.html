Fireplace Types

Fireplace Installation Options for Sacramento Homes

We install all types of fireplaces and heating inserts for Sacramento homeowners.

Wood-Burning Inserts

Convert an inefficient open fireplace into a high-efficiency wood-burning insert that heats your Sacramento home effectively. We handle liner installation, insert fitting, and all required permits.

Gas Fireplace Inserts

The most popular choice for Sacramento homeowners. Instant on/off, no wood needed, high efficiency, remote control capable. We install vented and direct-vent gas inserts with proper liner sizing for all Sacramento gas appliances.

New Masonry Fireplaces

Full new fireplace builds using traditional brick or stone masonry. Custom designs to complement your Sacramento home's architecture. Includes firebox, smoke chamber, flue liner, and chimney construction.

Zero-Clearance Fireplaces

Factory-built fireplaces that can be installed in homes without existing masonry. Cost-effective way to add a fireplace anywhere in your Sacramento home. Available in wood-burning and gas models.

Electric Fireplaces

No venting required. Electric inserts and built-in units can be installed in any room — ideal for Sacramento apartments, condos, or rooms without a chimney. Realistic flame effects and zone heating.

Pellet Stove Inserts

Highly efficient automated heating using compressed wood pellets. Easy to operate, consistent heat output, and EPA-approved. We install pellet inserts with proper venting for Sacramento homes.

FAQ

Fireplace Installation FAQs

How much does fireplace installation cost in Sacramento?+

Fireplace installation costs in Sacramento: wood insert $1,500–$4,000; gas insert $2,000–$5,000; zero-clearance unit $2,500–$6,000; new masonry fireplace $5,000–$20,000+; electric insert from $500. Costs include equipment, liner installation, permits, and labor. We provide free detailed estimates.

Do I need a permit for fireplace installation in Sacramento?+

Yes. All new fireplace and insert installations in Sacramento require building permits from Sacramento County or City of Sacramento. AL Chimney Service handles all permit applications as part of our installation service, ensuring full code compliance from start to finish.

What is the most efficient fireplace for a Sacramento home?+

Gas inserts are typically the most efficient option (70–90% efficiency vs. 10–20% for open wood-burning fireplaces). Pellet inserts also offer high efficiency (75–90%). For Sacramento's climate, gas inserts provide the best combination of efficiency, convenience, and heat output during the November–March heating season.

How long does fireplace installation take?+

A fireplace insert installation typically takes one full day (4–8 hours). A new masonry fireplace takes 3–7 days depending on design complexity. We always provide a clear timeline estimate before beginning work.
