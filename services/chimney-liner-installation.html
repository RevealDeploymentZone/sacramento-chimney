About Chimney Liners

What Is a Chimney Liner and Why Does It Matter?

A chimney liner is a conduit inside your chimney that safely contains and vents combustion gases and heat from your fireplace or heating appliance to the outside. It protects your home's structure from heat transfer and prevents combustion gases — including carbon monoxide — from leaking into living spaces.

California and Sacramento building codes require a properly sized, intact flue liner for all wood-burning fireplaces and gas appliances. Most Sacramento homes built before 1950 have clay tile liners that deteriorate with age, especially after chimney fires or years of seasonal use.

Types of Chimney Liners We Install

Stainless Steel Flexible Liners: The most popular choice for Sacramento homes. Can be installed in chimneys with bends and offsets. Available in rigid or flexible forms. Suitable for all fuel types. Comes with 20-year manufacturer warranty.

Cast-in-Place Liners: Ideal for older Sacramento masonry chimneys with irregular shapes. A poured insulating compound creates a new smooth liner inside the existing flue. Excellent for structural stabilization.

Aluminum Liners: Used for Category I gas appliances only. Lightweight and cost-effective for simple gas vent conversions.

When You Need a New Liner

Signs Your Sacramento Chimney Needs Relining

Cracked or spalling clay tiles

Level 2 video inspection reveals tile damage requiring relining.

Previous chimney fire

Chimney fires reach 2,000°F+ and routinely crack clay tile liners.

Converting from oil to gas

Gas appliances require a properly sized liner — often smaller than oil systems.

Home built before 1950

Many older Sacramento homes have unlined chimneys or severely deteriorated liners.

Carbon monoxide detected

CO presence is an emergency sign of liner failure requiring immediate relining.

Adding a wood stove or insert

New inserts typically require a properly sized stainless steel liner for safe operation.

FAQ

Chimney Liner FAQs

How much does chimney liner installation cost in Sacramento?+

Chimney liner installation in Sacramento costs $800–$3,000+ installed. A standard 6" stainless steel liner for a wood-burning fireplace in a typical two-story Sacramento home runs $1,200–$2,500. Gas appliance liners are typically less expensive. We provide free estimates with no obligation.

How long does a stainless steel chimney liner last?+

Quality stainless steel chimney liners (316L alloy for oil/gas, 304 for wood) last 20–35+ years with annual inspections. Most come with a manufacturer warranty of 20–25 years. This far outlasts clay tile liners, which typically last 10–25 years before requiring relining.

Is chimney liner installation a DIY job?+

No. Chimney liner installation requires precise sizing to match your appliance's BTU output, correct materials for your fuel type, and proper top and bottom terminations — all factors with direct safety implications. Improper installation can cause carbon monoxide poisoning or chimney fires. AL Chimney Service Sacramento handles all liner work to CSIA and California building code standards.

Can I use my fireplace with a cracked liner?+

No. A cracked liner allows heat and combustion gases including carbon monoxide to escape into your home's structure, creating both a fire and CO poisoning hazard. Stop using the fireplace immediately and call us at +1 916 694 1518 for an inspection and relining estimate.
