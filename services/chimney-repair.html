Repair Services

Chimney Repairs We Perform in Sacramento

We repair all types of chimney damage for Sacramento homes — from minor mortar work to full rebuilds.

Chimney Crown Repair

Cracked or crumbling chimney crowns allow water to penetrate during Sacramento's rainy season. We repair or rebuild crowns using a flexible, waterproof crown coating that lasts 15+ years.

Flue Liner Repair

Cracked clay tile liners can be resurfaced using HeatShield® or similar compounds. Severely damaged liners require full replacement with a stainless steel liner system.

Spalling Brick Repair

Spalling (flaking) brick is caused by moisture penetrating porous masonry and freezing. We replace damaged bricks and apply waterproof sealer to prevent future deterioration.

Damper Repair & Replacement

Broken, rusted, or stuck dampers reduce draft efficiency and allow outside air in. We repair or replace throat dampers and install energy-efficient top-mount dampers.

Chimney Flashing Repair

Leaking flashing — the metal seal between chimney and roof — causes water damage to rafters, insulation, and ceilings. We reseal or replace flashing to eliminate roof leaks.

Firebox Repair

Cracked firebox walls and floors are a serious safety issue. We repair or reline fireboxes using refractory mortar and firebrick to restore structural integrity and fire safety.

Warning Signs

Signs Your Sacramento Chimney Needs Repair

White staining (efflorescence)

Salt deposits on brick indicate water is moving through your masonry.

Water in the firebox after rain

Water penetration indicates damaged crown, cap, flashing, or masonry.

Crumbling or missing mortar

Deteriorated mortar joints allow moisture and carbon monoxide to escape.

Rust on damper or firebox

Rust is a sign of chronic moisture problems that need immediate attention.

Smoke entering the room

Draft problems often indicate structural chimney damage or blockages.

Visible cracks in chimney exterior

Cracks in the chimney stack allow water intrusion and signal structural issues.

FAQ

Chimney Repair FAQs

How much does chimney repair cost in Sacramento?+

Chimney repair costs in Sacramento depend on the type and extent of damage: minor mortar repairs $150–$300; crown repair $200–$500; flue liner resurfacing $400–$1,200; full liner replacement $800–$3,000+; partial rebuilds $1,500–$5,000+. We provide free itemized estimates before any work begins.

What causes chimney damage in Sacramento?+

Sacramento's climate is a primary factor. Rainy winters followed by hot, dry summers cause masonry to expand and contract, cracking mortar and brick. Rain infiltration during the wet season accelerates deterioration. Freeze events, though rare, can cause sudden spalling. Deferred maintenance is also a leading cause of progressive chimney damage.

Is it safe to use a damaged chimney?+

No. A damaged chimney can allow combustion gases including carbon monoxide to enter your home, and can create fire pathways through cracked liners. If you notice any signs of chimney damage, stop using the fireplace and call AL Chimney Service Sacramento at +1 916 694 1518 for an immediate inspection.

How long does chimney repair take?+

Minor repairs like damper replacement or mortar joint repair can be completed in 1–3 hours. Chimney crown repair or liner resurfacing typically takes a full day. Major repairs like partial rebuilds or full liner replacement may take 1–3 days. We provide a clear timeline estimate before beginning work.

Can I repair my chimney myself?+

We strongly advise against DIY chimney repairs. Improper repairs can create dangerous conditions — improperly mixed refractory mortar can fail under heat, and incorrect liner repairs can lead to carbon monoxide leaks. Chimney repair requires CSIA knowledge of materials, heat ratings, and building codes.
