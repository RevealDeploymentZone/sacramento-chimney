About This Service

What Is Chimney Cleaning?

Chimney cleaning — also called chimney sweeping — is the removal of creosote, soot, ash, and debris from your chimney's flue liner, firebox, smoke shelf, and smoke chamber. It is the single most important maintenance task for any wood-burning fireplace or stove.

When wood burns, it releases combustion gases that cool as they rise through the flue. This cooling creates condensation that deposits creosote — a dark, flammable tar-like substance — on the inner walls of your chimney. Over time, creosote builds up in three stages, with Stage 3 (glazed creosote) being extremely difficult to remove and a leading cause of chimney fires.

In Sacramento, homeowners use their fireplaces heavily during the cool November–March season. This concentrated burning season accelerates creosote buildup, making annual cleaning before fall essential for every Sacramento household with a wood-burning fireplace.

Why Sacramento Homes Need Annual Chimney Cleaning

Sacramento's Central Valley climate creates unique chimney challenges. Cooler, wetter winters mean more fireplace use. The combination of temperature swings and moisture promotes faster creosote accumulation and can introduce debris and animal nesting into unprotected chimneys. Warning Signs

Signs Your Chimney Needs Cleaning

Don't wait for a chimney fire. These signs mean it's time to call AL Chimney Service Sacramento.

Oily, tar-like smell from fireplace

Strong creosote odor — especially in summer — indicates heavy buildup inside the flue.

Smoke entering the room

Smoke backdraft often means a partially blocked or dirty flue with poor draft.

Black soot around the damper

Visible black soot or debris falling into your firebox means the flue needs cleaning.

Fireplace burns poorly or slowly

Reduced airflow from creosote buildup makes fires hard to start and maintain.

More than 12 months since last cleaning

Annual cleaning is the NFPA 211 standard — if it's been over a year, it's time.

Animals or nesting sounds

Birds and squirrels nesting in the flue block airflow and create fire hazards.

FAQ

Chimney Cleaning FAQs

Common questions Sacramento homeowners ask about chimney cleaning.

How much does chimney cleaning cost in Sacramento?+

Standard chimney cleaning in Sacramento costs $100–$200. Factors that affect price include the degree of creosote buildup (light, moderate, or heavy), chimney height, number of flues, and access difficulty. We always provide a free estimate before any work begins.

How often should chimneys be cleaned in Sacramento?+

The NFPA 211 standard requires annual chimney inspection and cleaning. In Sacramento, we recommend scheduling before October — before the fall/winter burning season begins. Heavy users who burn wood more than 3 nights per week should consider twice-yearly cleanings.

What does a chimney sweep include?+

A standard chimney sweep includes removal of creosote and soot from the entire flue liner, smoke chamber, and smoke shelf; cleaning of the firebox; and a visual inspection of accessible areas. We also provide a written condition report after every service.

Will chimney cleaning make a mess in my home?+

No. We use industrial HEPA vacuum equipment connected directly to your firebox opening and lay drop cloths to protect your floors and furniture. After cleaning, the work area is left as clean as we found it.

Is creosote dangerous if left in the chimney?+

Yes. Creosote is highly flammable. Stage 3 glazed creosote can ignite at temperatures chimney fires regularly reach (over 2,000°F), causing a dangerous chimney fire that can spread to your roof and home structure. The Chimney Safety Institute of America (CSIA) considers creosote a serious fire hazard that must be removed regularly.

Do you clean gas fireplace chimneys too?+

Yes. Gas fireplaces still produce moisture and combustion byproducts that can deposit in the venting system. We clean and inspect gas fireplace vents, check for blockages, and ensure proper draft — all important for both efficiency and carbon monoxide safety.
