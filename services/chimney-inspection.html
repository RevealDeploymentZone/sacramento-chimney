Inspection Levels

Three Levels of Chimney Inspection

The CSIA and NFPA 211 define three inspection levels. We perform all three.

Level 1 Inspection

Recommended for annual maintenance. Covers all accessible areas of the chimney interior and exterior including the firebox, damper, smoke chamber, chimney cap, and exposed exterior masonry. Includes a basic visual inspection without special tools. Cost: $75–$150.

Level 2 Inspection

Required when buying or selling a home, after a chimney fire, or when changing fuel type. Includes everything in Level 1 plus video camera scanning of the entire flue interior. Reveals hidden cracks, gaps, and damage not visible to the naked eye. Cost: $150–$300.

Level 3 Inspection

The most comprehensive inspection. Required when a hazard is suspected that can't be confirmed with Level 1 or 2. May involve removing chimney components for access. Used after major structural events or suspected hidden damage. Cost: Varies by scope.

What We Inspect

Complete Chimney Inspection Checklist

Our CSIA-certified technicians inspect every component of your chimney system to ensure it meets NFPA 211 safety standards. Here's what a full inspection covers:

Interior Components

Firebox walls and floor, smoke shelf, smoke chamber corbeling, damper operation and seal, flue liner condition (tile, metal, or cast-in-place), and all accessible interior masonry joints.

Exterior Components

Chimney cap and spark arrester, chimney crown condition, exterior masonry and mortar joints, chimney flashing and counter-flashing, and the chimney structure above the roofline.

Video Camera Scanning (Level 2)

Our high-definition camera system transmits live video from inside your flue, revealing cracks, spalling tile, mortar joint gaps, and blockages invisible from above or below. You receive a recorded video of the entire scan.

Chimney Inspection FAQs

How much does a chimney inspection cost in Sacramento?+

A Level 1 chimney inspection in Sacramento costs $75–$150. A Level 2 inspection with video camera scanning costs $150–$300. Level 3 inspections are priced based on scope. All inspections include a written report. Call +1 916 694 1518 for a free estimate.

What is the difference between Level 1 and Level 2 chimney inspection?+

A Level 1 inspection covers accessible areas visually. A Level 2 inspection goes further — it includes video camera scanning of the entire flue interior, attic, basement, and crawl space if applicable. Level 2 is required for real estate transactions, after chimney fires, or when changing the heating appliance type.

Do I need a chimney inspection when buying a home in Sacramento?+

Yes — strongly recommended. A Level 2 inspection is the only way to assess the true internal condition of a chimney. Standard home inspectors do not perform Level 2 chimney inspections. Undisclosed chimney damage can cost thousands to repair after purchase.

How often should I get a chimney inspection in Sacramento?+

Annually, per NFPA 211 standards. For Sacramento homeowners, the best time is early fall (September–October) before the fireplace season begins. Additional inspections are warranted after chimney fires, severe storms, or if you notice smoke backdraft or unusual odors.

Can I combine a chimney inspection with cleaning?+

Yes, and it's the most cost-effective approach. We offer combined cleaning and Level 1 inspection packages. Contact us for current pricing on bundled services in Sacramento.
