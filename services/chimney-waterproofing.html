Why Waterproof?

Why Sacramento Chimneys Need Waterproofing

Masonry is inherently porous. Brick can absorb up to one cup of water per hour during heavy rainfall. In Sacramento, where the wet season brings 18–20 inches of rain concentrated November through April, unprotected chimney masonry absorbs significant water every winter.

This absorbed water migrates inward, causing rust on metal components, spalling of brick faces, crumbling mortar, and deterioration of flue liners. The Chimney Safety Institute of America (CSIA) estimates that water is the #1 cause of chimney deterioration — more damaging than heat, creosote, or even chimney fires.

For Sacramento homes, chimney waterproofing is especially critical because of the climate pattern: wet winters followed by hot, dry summers. This dramatic moisture cycling causes masonry to repeatedly expand and contract, accelerating structural breakdown in ways that aren't visible until expensive damage has already occurred.

Why Penetrating Sealants (Not Paint)

The critical distinction: chimney waterproofing must use vapor-permeable sealants, not paint or non-permeable coatings. Chimneys must be able to release water vapor from combustion and internal moisture. Trapping this vapor with a non-permeable coating causes moisture to accumulate inside the masonry — significantly worsening damage.

AL Chimney Service Sacramento uses ChimneySaver Water Repellent and comparable professional-grade penetrating sealants that block liquid water entry while allowing vapor to escape. These products penetrate 1/4" into masonry pores rather than forming a surface film, providing lasting protection that won't peel or bubble.

FAQ

Chimney Waterproofing FAQs

How much does chimney waterproofing cost in Sacramento?+

Chimney waterproofing in Sacramento costs $150–$500 depending on chimney size and condition. The treatment is one of the most cost-effective investments for Sacramento homeowners — preventing thousands of dollars in moisture damage. We provide free estimates and often bundle waterproofing with tuckpointing.

How often should I waterproof my chimney in Sacramento?+

Professional-grade chimney waterproofing lasts 8–10 years in Sacramento's climate. We recommend reapplication every 8–10 years as part of regular chimney maintenance, or sooner if you notice signs of water infiltration like efflorescence, interior moisture, or rust on metal components.

Can I use regular masonry sealer on my chimney?+

No. Regular masonry sealers and paints are not vapor-permeable — they trap moisture inside the chimney structure, making damage worse. Only use products specifically formulated for chimneys that allow water vapor transmission while blocking liquid water. AL Chimney Service uses ChimneySaver and similar CSIA-recommended professional products.

Should I waterproof my chimney before or after tuckpointing?+

Tuckpointing must be completed before waterproofing. New mortar needs to cure for at least 7 days before sealant is applied. We commonly perform both services together — tuckpoint deteriorated joints, allow to cure, then apply waterproof treatment for comprehensive, long-lasting protection.
