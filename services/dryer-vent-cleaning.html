Why It Matters

Dryer Vent Cleaning: A Critical Home Safety Service

Your clothes dryer works by heating air and pushing it through your laundry and out the exhaust vent. With every cycle, tiny lint fibers are carried into the vent duct. Over months and years, this lint accumulates — reducing airflow, forcing your dryer to work harder, and creating a dangerous combustible buildup inside the vent.

According to the U.S. Fire Administration, dryers cause approximately 2,900 residential fires annually, resulting in an estimated $35 million in property losses. The leading cause: failure to clean the dryer vent. Lint is extremely combustible — it's the same material used as fire starter in camping equipment.

Energy Savings & Dryer Longevity

Beyond fire safety, a blocked dryer vent significantly increases drying time and energy costs. A dryer with a partially blocked vent can use 20–30% more energy per cycle. Regular cleaning can cut your drying time in half and extend the life of your dryer by several years. For Sacramento homeowners, annual dryer vent cleaning pays for itself quickly in reduced energy bills.

Warning Signs

Signs Your Dryer Vent Needs Cleaning

Clothes take 2+ cycles to dry

Longest warning sign — severely restricted airflow forces multiple drying cycles.

Dryer is very hot to the touch

Overheating indicates that heat can't escape through a blocked vent.

Burning smell during use

A burning smell is a serious warning — stop the dryer and call us immediately.

Vent flap not opening outside

The outdoor vent flap should fully open during dryer operation.

More than 12 months since last cleaning

Annual cleaning is the NFPA standard for dryer vent maintenance.

Laundry room feels humid

Excess moisture in the laundry room indicates restricted vent exhaust.

FAQ

Dryer Vent Cleaning FAQs

How much does dryer vent cleaning cost in Sacramento?+

Dryer vent cleaning in Sacramento costs $80–$175 depending on vent length, number of bends, and blockage severity. We provide free estimates and offer discounts when combined with chimney cleaning services.

How often should I get my dryer vent cleaned?+

The NFPA recommends annual dryer vent cleaning. Households with large families or frequent laundry should clean every 6 months. Homes with longer vent runs (over 15 feet) or multiple bends need more frequent service as lint accumulates faster.

Is dryer vent cleaning really necessary?+

Yes — it's a critical fire safety service. The U.S. Fire Administration reports 2,900 dryer fires annually. Lint buildup is the leading cause. Regular cleaning also restores dryer efficiency, reduces drying time, and extends appliance life. It's one of the most cost-effective home maintenance services available.

Can you clean dryer vents that go through the roof?+

Yes. We clean dryer vents regardless of routing — whether they exhaust through the wall, roof, or crawl space. We use flexible rod systems that navigate bends and extended runs. Roof-exhausting vents are common in Sacramento condos and townhomes and require special equipment we carry on every service call.
