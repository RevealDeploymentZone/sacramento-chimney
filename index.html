FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything Sacramento homeowners ask about chimney cleaning and maintenance.

How often should I have my chimney cleaned in Sacramento? +

The NFPA 211 standard recommends chimney inspection and cleaning at least once per year. For Sacramento homeowners who use their fireplace regularly during the November–March cool season, we suggest scheduling your cleaning in September or October before the burning season begins.

What does your chimney inspection include? +

Our CSIA-certified inspection covers the firebox, smoke chamber, damper, flue liner, chimney crown, chimney cap, and exterior masonry. We check for creosote buildup, cracks, blockages, animal nesting, and structural damage. We offer Level 1, 2, and 3 inspections per CSIA standards, including video camera scanning for a thorough internal view.

Do you offer emergency chimney services in Sacramento? +

Yes! AL Chimney Service Sacramento operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year — including all holidays. Whether it's a chimney fire, carbon monoxide concern, collapsed flue, or dangerous creosote buildup, call us immediately at +1 916 694 1518. Emergency response is our priority.

How much does chimney cleaning cost in Sacramento? +

Basic chimney sweeping in Sacramento typically ranges from $100–$200 for a standard cleaning. Prices vary based on the level of creosote buildup, chimney height, and accessibility. We provide free, no-obligation estimates before starting any work. There are no hidden fees or surprise charges — ever.

What are signs I need chimney repair in Sacramento? +

Watch for these warning signs: white staining (efflorescence) on brick, crumbling mortar joints, damaged or cracked chimney crown, rust on the firebox or damper, water in the firebox after rain, spalling brickwork, or a strong odor coming from the fireplace. Sacramento's seasonal rains can accelerate moisture damage — don't ignore these signs.

Why does my fireplace smoke when I use it? +

A smoking fireplace in Sacramento is usually caused by a blocked or dirty flue, a closed damper, incorrect flue sizing, negative air pressure in the home, or a downdraft issue. We diagnose and fix all smoking fireplace problems — call us for a same-day assessment at +1 916 694 1518.

Do you clean dryer vents in Sacramento? +

Yes. Dryer vent cleaning is one of our most requested services. Lint buildup in dryer vents is a leading cause of home fires — the U.S. Fire Administration reports nearly 3,000 dryer fires annually. We clean and inspect residential and commercial dryer vent systems throughout Sacramento and surrounding areas.
